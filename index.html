About Potato Team Games

Potato Team Games makes free, fun browser games that run on any device — PC, Mac, Chromebook, iPhone and Android. Every game works right in your browser with no download, no account and no ads. Your progress is saved automatically on your device. Join the Potato Team Games Discord to meet other players.

Our newest game, Potato Ville, is a cozy virtual pet game inspired by the classic Facebook game PetVille: create a potato animal, dress it up, decorate your home in build mode, work mini-game jobs, shop in town and make friends with the neighbours. Friends on the same Wi-Fi can walk around town together and visit each other's homes. Play in English or Danish.

Two new games are in early access and still in development: Potato Pool is a 2D 8-ball pool game with a campaign through eight cities, AI rivals, a trophy room full of cups to collect, daily and weekly quests and Wi-Fi play with friends. UNO Potato is a potato-themed card game on a real-looking table – match colours, stack Double Fries and shout SPUD! against the computer or up to four friends on the same Wi-Fi.

The Potato Empire is a classic property trading board game that looks like it's sitting on your table. Roll the dice, buy streets, build houses and hotels and trade your way to the top – against the computer, with friends on one device, or on your own devices over the same Wi-Fi. Play in English or Danish.

Potato Bingo Adventures is a bingo game with a twist: win bingo to build towns all around the world, from Ireland to the Moon and beyond.

Our first game, Potato Clicker, is an idle clicker game like Cookie Clicker — but with potatoes, cowboys and a whole lot of upgrades.
