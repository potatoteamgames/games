About Potato Team Games

Potato Team Games makes free, fun browser games that run on any device — PC, Mac, Chromebook, iPhone and Android. Every game works right in your browser with no download, no account and no ads. Your progress is saved automatically on your device.

Our newest game, Potato Bingo Adventures, is a bingo game with a twist: win bingo to build towns all around the world, from Ireland to the Moon and beyond.

Our first game, Potato Clicker, is an idle clicker game like Cookie Clicker — but with potatoes, cowboys and a whole lot of upgrades.
