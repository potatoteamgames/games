🥔 About Potato Clicker

Potato Clicker is a free idle clicker game set in the Wild West. Click the giant potato, earn potatoes and build a potato empire — from a tiny peeler to steam trains, gold mines and galaxy farms. No download and no sign-up: it runs right in your browser on PC, Mac, Chromebook, iPhone and Android.

How to play

  • Click the potato to earn potatoes. Fast clicks build a combo bonus.
  • Buy machines that make potatoes for you, even while you're not clicking.
  • Unlock 300+ upgrades that multiply your clicks and production.
  • Catch golden potatoes and falling potato rain for big bonuses.
  • Watch the weather — rain, thunderstorms, snow, heatwaves and dust storms change the game, and trains, bandits and UFOs sometimes show up.
  • Complete missions, spin the lucky wheel and open your daily chest.
  • Level up, collect 350+ trophies and rebirth for permanent power.

Features

Endless machines, skins, hats and backgrounds, boosts you can save for later, a soul shop with perks, and automatic saving. Like Cookie Clicker — but with potatoes and cowboys.

Is it free?

Yes. Potato Clicker is 100% free to play with no ads and no in-app purchases. Your progress is saved in your browser.

🧭 Extract – next world

Reach level 100 and pay a big pile of potatoes to Extract. You win a medal and travel to a brand new world with a new look – but everything else starts over from zero. Each medal gives x2 production and clicks forever. 4 medals to collect!

✨ Rebirth – same world

👻 Potato Souls build up from all the potatoes you have ever earned (first soul at 10 B). Rebirth collects them: you start over with potatoes, machines and upgrades, and your level goes back to 1 – but each soul gives +5% to everything forever. Every 10 souls also gives 1 🪙 Soul Coin (at least 1 per Rebirth). You keep your style, trophies and bag. Spend Soul Coins in the 🪙 Soul Shop at the bottom of 🛒 Shop.
